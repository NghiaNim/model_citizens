The 2026 United States Senate election in South Dakota will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect a member of the United States Senate to represent the state of South Dakota. Republican incumbent Mike Rounds is seeking a third term. He is being challenged by independent Air Force officer Brian Bengs.

Primary elections were held on June 2. Rounds received 75.8% of the vote against minimal opposition. Former state trooper Julian Beaudion won the Democratic nomination unopposed, but withdrew from the race on August 4.[1] Bengs, the Democratic nominee in 2022, filed to run as an independent.

Republicans have not lost a Senate election in South Dakota since 2008. The last time an independent was elected to South Dakota's Senate seat was in 1891.

Background

[edit]

South Dakota is considered a red state.[2] As of October 2026[update] voter registration was 331,873 Republican, 157,608 indepedent/non-affiliated, 135,324 Democratic, and 2796 Libertarian.[3] Democrats have not won a Senate race or statewide election in South Dakota since Tim Johnson was re-elected in 2008.[4][5]

The state is one of three (along with Nebraska and Idaho) in 2026 Senate election cycle where the Democratic nominee has withdrawn from the race, effectively clearing the field for an independent.[6]
